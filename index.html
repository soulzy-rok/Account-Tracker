Welcome, here is how it works

  1. In Rise of Kingdoms open your profile and tap the “i” next to Kill Points.
  2. Take a screenshot of the Kill Statistics popup.
  3. Drop the screenshot into the box below, or paste it with Ctrl+V.
  4. Check the numbers and press Save snapshot. After your next fights, repeat it to see your gains.

Theme

Reading accuracy

Default: free OCR (Tesseract.js) in your browser. For much better reading, add an Anthropic API key. It is stored only in this browser and requests go directly to Anthropic, so use it only on your own device. Usage is billed to your key.

Your data

Everything (snapshots, showcase, images and settings) is kept in a database inside this browser and is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data erases it, so download a backup now and then. Your API key is not included in backups.

Help

  • Use the four pages at the top (bottom on a phone), or press 1 to 4.
  • You can paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V from any page.
  • Press Enter in a number field to save the snapshot.
  • On the Showcase page, click any part of the card to edit it. Add images by clicking a tile, dragging a file onto it, or hovering it and pressing Ctrl+V.
  • Deleted or just-saved snapshots can be undone from the message that appears.
  • Open the Kill Statistics popup by tapping the “i” next to Kill Points on your profile.
